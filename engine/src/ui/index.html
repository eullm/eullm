      Sampling

      0.70 0 always picks the likeliest token; higher is more varied.
      0.90 Draws only from the likeliest tokens that add up to this share.
      40 Draws only from this many likeliest tokens; 0 turns it off.
      0.00 Drops tokens less likely than this share of the likeliest one.
      1.10 Above 1 makes words just used less likely; 1 turns it off.
