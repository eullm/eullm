# EULLM Forge — R&D Roadmap: distillation & verticalization

**Baseline:** Forge pipeline v0.x (prune → distill → quant → identity LoRA → GGUF) · July 2026
**Purpose:** decide the distillation recipe for our verticalized models *empirically*
(starting with `legal-it-7b`) instead of by gut feeling.

Operational document: every item has a checkbox — `[ ]` → `[x]` on completion. An item is
"done" only when it meets the Definition of Done at the bottom.

---

## Binding principles
- **Apache-2.0 teacher** (or a compatible permissive licence). No Llama in the default catalog.
- **KL only with an identical tokenizer**: teacher and student from the same family/vocab
  (Qwen3 ↔ Qwen3), otherwise logit-level KL is undefined.
- **Dense, not MoE** for KL (routing introduces logit variance that hurts distillation).
- **Single-GPU 94–96 GB** as the reference constraint (teacher + student co-resident in VRAM).
  Note: a frozen teacher is loaded quantized — e.g. a 70B at 4-bit is ~35 GB, *lighter* than a
  30B at FP16 (~60 GB).
- **GDPR-safe dataset**: always the anonymized corpus (`anonymize.py`), never raw data.
- **Isolate variables**: change one thing at a time (teacher size / method), same dataset,
  same student init, same budget, same seed where possible.
- **Eval before models**: no comparison without a yardstick.

---

## F0 — Evaluation harness *(HARD PREREQUISITE — blocking)*
**Exit gate:** a reproducible, blind way to rank two Italian-legal models exists.

- [ ] **F0-A · Italian legal held-out set** — 200–500 questions/prompts with reference answers or
  a scoring rubric (civil, criminal, GDPR, procedure). Outside the training set. Versioned.
- [ ] **F0-B · Metrics** — (1) perplexity on held-out legal text (coarse, cheap);
  (2) QA accuracy; (3) **LLM-as-judge** (strong model, **blind** A-vs-B scoring, explicit rubric);
  (4) human spot-check by a lawyer on a sample (~30–50 items).
- [ ] **F0-C · Reusability** — the harness is parametric by domain/language → reusable for
  `medical-de` and `finance-fr`. It must stand on its own as an asset, not just serve legal.

---

## F1 — Comparative pilot *(at REDUCED scale)*
**Exit gate:** an honest directional signal on which recipe wins, at minimum cost.

Arms (same anonymized dataset, same student `Qwen3-7B-Base`, identical budget):
- [ ] **A · 32B + KL** — teacher `Qwen3-32B` (FP8), logit-level distillation. *"Clean-signal" baseline.*
- [ ] **B · 32B + SeqKD** — same teacher, but sequence-level (SFT on generated text). → **isolates the METHOD.**
- [ ] **C · 70B + SeqKD** — 70B-4bit teacher (Apache), sequence-level. → **isolates teacher SIZE** (vs B).
- [ ] **H · Hybrid** *(optional, often the winner)* — 32B in KL **+** synthetic data generated by
  the 70B mixed into the training set. Best of both worlds.

Notes:
- [ ] Run at **reduced scale** (dataset slice / fewer steps) for the signal, not final models.
- [ ] Budget the **synthetic-corpus generation** with the 70B separately (the hidden cost of SeqKD/H).
- [ ] Record per arm: teacher, precision, method, hyperparameters, GPU hours, estimated cost.

**Decision gate F1 → F2:** pick **one** production recipe based on the F0 eval (not "by feel").
The other arms remain a **documented ablation**.

---

## F2 — Scale the winner → production `legal-it-7b`
**Exit gate:** an `eullm/legal-it-7b` Q4_K_M GGUF that beats base Qwen3-7B on the F0 eval,
with a Forge-generated compliance card.

- [ ] Full run of the winning recipe on the complete dataset.
- [ ] Optional identity LoRA (branding) after distillation.
- [ ] GGUF Q4_K_M export + local smoke-test via the Engine.
- [ ] **Per-model compliance card generated by Forge** (Annex XI §1 + training-content summary +
  copyright policy + provenance + intended use) — see `compliance-card-template`.

---

## F3 — Generalization
**Exit gate:** the winning recipe is a *function* of (domain, language), not a one-off.

- [ ] Apply the winning recipe to `medical-de-7b` and `finance-fr-7b` with the same eval harness.
- [ ] Consolidate the recipe into `pipeline.py` defaults and the profiles.
- [ ] Re-evaluate the 70B-4bit-SeqKD teacher as a quality upgrade (v0.2) if F1 points that way.

---

## Cross-cutting
- **Compliance is a by-product of the research.** The F1 ablation (design choices + rationale +
  evaluation) **populates** the Annex XI fields of the compliance card. The research *is* AI Act
  documentation.
- **Naming.** Internally `legal-it-7b-{kl,seqkd,hybrid}`; publicly a **single**
  `eullm/legal-it-7b` (the winner).
- **Budget.** Estimate cost/arm *before* F1; a reduced pilot ≪ two full models.
- **GPAI-provider risk (AI Act).** If a recipe crosses the ~1/3-of-base-compute threshold,
  document as a GPAI provider (already covered in the compliance dossier).

---

## Definition of Done (per item)
1. Reproducible: single command + versioned config, idempotent resume-from-checkpoint.
2. Measured: numbers on the F0 eval, not impressions.
3. Recorded: teacher / method / hyperparameters / hours / cost written to the model changelog.
4. GDPR-safe: anonymized corpus only; no raw data or PII in the weights (membership-inference probe).
