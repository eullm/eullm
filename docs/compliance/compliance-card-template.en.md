# EULLM Compliance Card — template, schema and example

> **What it is for.** It defines how the **compliance cards for EULLM verticalized models** are
> organized (e.g. `eullm/legal-it-7b`). The card **packages** the artifacts the AI Act requires
> (Annex XI §1, Art. 53(1)(c)(d), Art. 50) plus the delimitation of the use and the provenance, so
> that the **deployer/user** can discharge its **own** obligations.
>
> **What it is NOT.** It is not a conformity assessment, not a stamp, not a guarantee. The risk
> classification depends on the **use** the deployer makes of it.
>
> **Scope**: it applies to the verticalized models (in development), **not** to the models
> downloadable today. It replaces the Hub's current static/hardcoded cards (`hub/src/main.rs`).

---

## 1. Two-layer structure

- **Level A — GPAI Model Compliance Card** (ALWAYS, for every model): covers Annex XI §1 +
  training summary + copyright policy + transparency + intended use + provenance.
- **Level B — High-Risk Deployment Enablement Pack** (OPTIONAL, for sensitive verticals):
  pre-fills parts of Annex IV and Art. 26 **in support** of the deployer that uses the model in an
  Annex III context. The deployer remains the responsible party.

Each card exists in **two formats** generated together by Forge:
- `compliance-card.json` — machine-readable (Hub, RAG Enterprise, audit).
- `COMPLIANCE-CARD.md` — human-readable (legal, procurement).

---

## 2. Principles (differences from today's static card)

1. **Per-model, not static** — every field derives from the real provenance, **generated by
   Forge** at the end of the pipeline.
2. **Factual and qualified, never self-absolving** — fields like `gdpr_compliant: true`,
   `personal_data: "No personal data"`, hardcoded `systemic_risk: false` are forbidden. In their
   place, verifiable facts with references.
3. **Signed and anchored to integrity** — tied to the GGUF's SHA-256 digest, versioned.
4. **Explicit responsibility disclaimer** at the top of the document.

## 5. Generation checklist (Forge) — what the pipeline must emit

- [ ] `compliance-card.json` (schema v1) + `COMPLIANCE-CARD.md` alongside the GGUF.
- [ ] Real provenance: base model, license, transformation chain, GGUF SHA-256.
- [ ] `training_content_summary` with the published **official AI Office template** (Art. 53(1)(d)).
- [ ] `copyright_policy` with TDM opt-out (Art. 53(1)(c)).
- [ ] `personal_data` **factual** (never "no personal data" unless proven) + LIA reference.
- [ ] `systemic_risk` with a **FLOP estimate**, not a hardcoded boolean.
- [ ] `intended_purpose` with `not_intended` + `high_risk_triggers` mapped to Annex III.
- [ ] Responsibility disclaimer at the top.
- [ ] Card signature/hash; version.
- [ ] (If a sensitive vertical) Level B — High-Risk Enablement Pack.
